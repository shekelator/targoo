Hallelujah! For it is good to sing praise to our God — how pleasant, how fitting, is a song of praise.
The LORD builds up Jerusalem, gathers the outcasts of Israel.
He heals the brokenhearted and binds up their wounds.
He counts the number of the stars, calling each of them by name.
Great is our Lord, and mighty in power; his understanding has no limit.
The LORD lifts up the humble and casts the wicked to the ground.
Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make music to our God on the harp.
He covers the sky with clouds, prepares rain for the earth, and makes grass grow on the hills.
He gives food to the animals, to the young ravens when they call.
He takes no pleasure in the strength of a horse, nor delight in the legs of a warrior.
The LORD delights in those who fear him, in those who hope in his steadfast love.
Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem; glorify your God, O Zion.
For he has strengthened the bars of your gates and blessed your children within you.
He brings peace to your borders and satisfies you with the finest wheat.
He sends his word to the earth; his command runs swiftly.
He spreads snow like wool and scatters frost like ashes.
He hurls down his hail like pebbles — who can stand before his cold?
He sends out his word and melts them; he makes the wind blow, and the waters flow.
He declares his word to Jacob, his statutes and judgments to Israel.
He has not done this for any other nation; they do not know his judgments. Hallelujah!
