Hallelujah! Praise God in his sanctuary; praise him in his mighty expanse.
Praise him for his mighty deeds; praise him for his surpassing greatness.
Praise him with the blast of the shofar; praise him with lute and lyre.
Praise him with tambourine and dance; praise him with strings and pipe.
Praise him with resounding cymbals; praise him with clashing cymbals.
Let everything that has breath praise the LORD. Hallelujah!
